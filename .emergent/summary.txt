<product_requirements>
The Mithaq app is an Arabic-localized digital marriage counseling tool based on CBCT and ABA principles. It targets Arabic-speaking couples to improve communication.
**Core Features:**
*   **Synchronized Accounts:** Securely links two user accounts with a pairing code; data is viewable by both.
*   **A-B-C Behavioral Analyzer:** Users log Antecedent (what happened before tension), Behavior (negative action), and Consequence (immediate result). The system identifies recurring A-B-C chains.
*   **Positive Reinforcement & Skills System:**
    *   **Reinforcement Bank:** Private list of personalized reinforcers (e.g., Five minutes uninterrupted listening), visible only to the partner.
    *   **Token Economy System:** Partners award Quick Positive Points for desired behaviors. Points can redeem reinforcers.
*   **Educational Content (Skills Training):** Micro-learning modules (e.g., Time-Out and Self-Soothing, Active Listening, Expressing Needs). Behavioral homework prompts real-life application.
*   **Immediate Repair Cycle:** Upon logging a negative behavior, the offender completes a 3-step pop-up (acknowledge, transfer points, complete skill section), and the recipient receives confirmation and mandatory skill learning.
*   **Role-Based Notifications:** Differentiated notifications are sent to the offender and recipient after a negative A-B-C log, directing them to relevant skills.
*   **Arabic UI/UX:** Full localization, RTL layout, proper typography, and cultural adaptations.
*   **Data Security:** End-to-end encryption and high standards for sensitive marital data.
</product_requirements>
